Priorities, phasing, and timeline. Sequenced to prove value fast in Phase 1 before scaling investment.

## Guiding Principles

- **Prove before you scale.** Phase 1 is a real pilot with real teams, not a demo. Nothing moves to Phase 2 without validated pilot output.
- **Data before agents.** The agents are only as good as the Needs Bank and Offer Bank. Seeding those correctly is the highest-priority task.
- **Kill complexity early.** The kill filter exists to stop bad concepts fast. The build plan mirrors this.

## What We Are Not Building

- A mobile app
- A public-facing grower portal
- A custom LLM - using AWS Bedrock (Claude) as the inference layer
- A CRM replacement
