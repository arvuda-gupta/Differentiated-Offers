# Phase-wise Execution Plan

**Document Purpose**

This page defines the phased delivery plan for the Differentiated Offers platform. It covers three phases from Foundation POC through Enterprise Platform, with detailed deliverables, risks, dependencies, and open questions.

## Section 1 - Phase Overview Summary

| Phase | Name | Duration | Core Deliverables | Agents Live | Success / Exit Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Phase 1** | Foundation POC | ~3 months | Needs Bank seeded, Offer Bank seeded, Discovery + Validation agentic loop, Workspace UI for Stages 1 - 2, pilot with 2 commercial teams | Orchestrator Agent, Discovery Agent, Concept Validation Agent | 2 pilot teams completed Stages 1 - 2 with agents; differentiation gate working; kill filter catching weak concepts |
| **Phase 2** | Scaling Intelligence | ~4 months | Market Intelligence Agent, Scaling Agent, VENUS Databricks integration, Dashboard v1, full 6-stage workflow, rollout to additional teams | + Market Intelligence Agent, + Scaling Agent | ≥3 offers have scaling briefs generated; portfolio heatmap live; cross-country porting workflow validated |
| **Phase 3** | Enterprise Platform | ~5 months | Review Agent, full governance (RBAC, audit logs), Carbon/Sustainability (f) workflow, advanced analytics, pan-EU commercial rollout, annual review cycle | + Review Agent, + full analytics stack | >60% EU commercial teams onboarded; annual review cycle running; % of sales from differentiated offers tracked against 5YP target |

## Section 2 - Phase 1: Foundation POC (Detailed)

### 2.1 Objectives

- Prove the agentic loop works: Discovery Agent + Concept Validation Agent operating without manual data lookups.
- Seed the two core databases (Needs Bank + Offer Bank) with existing data from known Excel sources.
- Build the Workspace UI covering Stages 1 (Discovery) and 2 (Concept Development).
- Run with 2 real commercial teams from different regions - recommended: 1 Oceanic + 1 South East for contrast in offer maturity and archetype mix.
- Establish baseline metrics: time to problem statement, kill filter hit rate, differentiation gate pass rate.

### 2.4 Phase 1 Tech Spike: Orchestration Framework

**Decision Required in Week 1**

Before committing to an orchestration approach, a 2-week spike is required to evaluate the following options. The decision must be made in Week 1 of Phase 1.

| Framework | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AWS Step Functions** | Suited for deterministic state machines; strong AWS-native integration; managed service with audit trail | Less flexible for dynamic agent-to-agent communication; verbose definitions; not purpose-built for LLM flows |
| **LangGraph** | Purpose-built for multi-agent flows; supports cycles and parallel execution; Python-native; active ecosystem | Less mature operationally; requires custom monitoring integration |
| **Custom SQS event-driven** | Maximum flexibility; no framework lock-in | Maximum build cost; highest maintenance burden; not recommended |

**Recommendation:** LangGraph for agent orchestration logic + AWS SQS for async event triggers. Time-box the spike strictly - default to LangGraph if inconclusive.

### 2.5 Pilot Team Selection Criteria

- Must have an active offer concept in development (not hypothetical).
- At least one Oceanic region team and one South East region team - chosen for contrast in offer maturity and archetype mix.
- Team lead willing to log learnings into the platform (not just use it once and revert).
- Commercial unit with at least 3 entries in the current Offer Bank so the system has local data to surface.

### 2.6 Phase 1 Risks

**Phase 1 Risk Register**

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Needs Bank data quality insufficient - pain points too generic for agent use | Medium | High | Run a 1-week SME review session to validate and enrich the 70 entries before going live with agents. |
| Orchestration framework spike takes >2 weeks | Medium | Medium | Time-box strictly; default to LangGraph if spike is inconclusive. |
| Pilot teams revert to Excel (change management failure) | High | High | Keep UI friction lower than current Excel process; designate a platform champion in each pilot team. |
| I-Plan data stale or misaligned with current market figures | Low | Medium | Cross-check against known market figures before import; flag discrepancies for SME review. |
| Discovery Agent returns too much data (information overload) | Medium | High | Cap output to top 3 needs + top 2 existing offers + 1 market data point. Additional results available on demand. |

## Section 3 - Phase 2: Scaling Intelligence (Detailed)

### 3.1 Objectives

- Activate Market Intelligence Agent - requires populated ConceptBank and Offer Bank from Phase 1 pilot.
- Activate Scaling Agent - requires ≥5 live offers in MRP DB.
- Connect VENUS Databricks live feed to replace the I-Plan import proxy used in Phase 1.
- Build Leadership Dashboard v1: portfolio heatmap, pipeline funnel, offer scoring table.
- Roll out to 5 - 8 additional commercial teams via structured onboarding programme.
- Execute first cross-country scaling attempt using Scaling Agent output.

### 3.2 New Deliverables (Phase 2)

| # | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Market Intelligence Agent - pattern detector, stagnation monitor, white space analyser, portfolio balance monitor |
| 2 | Scaling Agent - regional fit analysis, adaptation brief generator, CU capability registry lookup, prior learnings retrieval |
| 3 | VENUS Databricks connector (read-only API: market size + SYT share by crop/CU) |
| 5 | Leadership Dashboard v1 - portfolio heatmap (region × archetype), pipeline funnel (Discovery → Concept → Validation → Launch), offer scoring table, white space overlay |
| 6 | Full 6-stage Workspace UI (Stages 3 - 6 fully agentic: Stage 4 launch planning, Stage 5 scale-up workspace) |
| 7 | Offer Bank enrichment: Scaling Agent output fields (candidate market scores, adaptation complexity) |
| 8 | Cross-workspace aggregation queries for Market Intelligence Agent pattern detection |
| 9 | Expanded pilot: 5 - 8 teams, structured onboarding programme with feedback cadence |

### 3.3 Phase 2 Risks

**Phase 2 Risk Register**

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VENUS Databricks integration blocked by data governance | Medium | High | Pre-align with data governance team in Phase 1; use read-only service account; document data lineage requirements early. |
| Scaling Agent produces low-quality briefs (insufficient data volume) | Medium | Medium | Require minimum 10 live offers before Scaling Agent is activated; supplement with manual regional context in early briefs. |
| Market Intelligence Agent triggers alert fatigue | High | Medium | Strict relevance threshold: only surface patterns seen in ≥3 workspaces or stagnation >6 months. Alerts routed to platform champion first, not all team members. |
| Dashboard data latency unacceptable for leadership | Low | Low | Apache Superset scheduled refresh every 4 hours is sufficient for a leadership portfolio view; no real-time requirement. |

## Section 4 - Phase 3: Enterprise Platform (Detailed)

### 4.1 Objectives

- Activate Review Agent with annual review cycle running for all live offers.
- Implement full governance: proper RBAC with row-level security, audit logs for all DB mutations, data lineage tracking.
- Carbon/Sustainability (f) component - full workflow including MRV verification plan check and regulatory change feed.
- Finance & Trading (e) full compliance workflow including local legal review trigger and credit risk scoring.
- Pan-EU commercial rollout targeting >60% of EU commercial teams.
- Advanced analytics: offer lifetime value, ROI per offer, contribution to % of sales against 5YP target.
- Integrate with Syngenta's broader commercial stack (CRM, CPQ if applicable).

### 4.2 New Deliverables (Phase 3)

| # | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Review & Retirement Agent - annual scheduling, review brief generation, Continue / Evolve / Archive decision workflow |
| 2 | Full RBAC: Offer Owner / Custodian / Steward / Stakeholder roles with row-level security on MRP DB by region/CU |
| 3 | Audit log: all DB writes captured with user, timestamp, before and after values |
| 4 | Carbon Add-on (f) workflow: MRV verification plan template, regulatory change feed integration, carbon registry standards check |
| 5 | Finance/Trading (e) compliance workflow: local legal review trigger, credit risk scoring integration, DSO/AR health KPI tracking |
| 6 | Advanced analytics layer: offer lifetime value model, portfolio ROI, % of EU revenue from differentiated offers (the 5YP ambition metric) |
| 7 | CRM integration (if applicable): offer workspace linked to account/opportunity records |
| 8 | Pan-EU onboarding programme: structured training, regional champions programme, Confluence documentation updates |
| 9 | Process comparison matrix completion: Crop Protection / Seeds / Agronomy / Technology offer development process comparison (currently a blank template) |

### 4.3 Phase 3 Risks

**Phase 3 Risk Register**

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon/f verification complexity blocks overall Phase 3 rollout | High | Medium | Phase the (f) component as an optional add-on; gate MRV requirement to only offers explicitly claiming carbon credits. Remainder of Phase 3 is not blocked. |
| Pan-EU change management - adoption falls short of 60% target | High | High | Regional champions programme; executive sponsorship from (Paris offsite sponsors); publish and communicate wins from Phase 2 to build adoption momentum ahead of Phase 3 rollout. |
| % of sales target not defined before Phase 3 analytics build begins | Medium | High | The Feb 2026 offsite ambition workshop (“3 breakout groups propose 5YP ambition”) must produce a concrete number before Phase 3 advanced analytics are meaningful. This must be chased as a Phase 1 pre-requisite action. |

## Section 6 - Open Questions

**Open Questions - To Be Resolved Before Phase 1 Start**

1. **Pilot team selection:** Which 2 commercial teams will participate in the Phase 1 pilot? Who owns the decision? *Recommended: 1 Oceanic region + 1 South East region.*
2. **5YP % of sales ambition:** Is the Feb 2026 offsite ambition number documented anywhere? If not, who owns capturing and socialising it? This is a blocker for Phase 3 analytics.
3. **Orchestration spike ownership:** LangGraph vs AWS Step Functions - who runs the 2-week spike? Which engineer/team lead owns the decision output?
4. **VENUS Databricks access:** Has a read-only service account been requested from the data governance team? Who is the named data owner for the VENUS dataset?
5. **I-Plan data ownership:** Who owns the initial I-Plan export and validation for import into the Needs Bank context? What is the agreed cadence for refreshing this data in Phase 1?
6. **Process comparison matrix:** Who fills in the Crop Protection / Seeds / Agronomy / Technology columns? This is currently a blank template in the Confluence space.
7. **Discovery Meetings Confluence page:** The page is currently empty. Where do the field research outputs from the 2025 working groups live? These need to be centralised before Needs Bank enrichment can begin.
